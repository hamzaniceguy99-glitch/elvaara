How we work

A coach, not a yarn shop

We sell no yarn, no patterns and no needles, and take no commission from anyone who does. That means we can tell you the cheaper yarn is fine for this project, or that you already own a suitable needle.

What you get is someone who looks at photos of your actual stitches every week. Most problems — puckering, drifting tension, a yoke that gapes — are obvious in a close-up and invisible in a tutorial.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

I'm completely new. Where do I start?

The Learn to knit program starts with holding the needles. By week eight most people have finished a hat they'd actually wear.

Knitting or crochet — which is easier?

Crochet is usually faster to pick up and works up quicker; knitting makes stretchier fabric for garments. We can start you on either.

Do you sell yarn or patterns?

No. We'll tell you what to look for in a yarn and what a pattern needs to include, but you buy from wherever you like — and you should buy patterns rather than sharing copies.

I'm left-handed.

Not a problem. We teach both directions, and plenty of left-handed knitters work the standard way comfortably.

My tension is all over the place.

Extremely common and very fixable. It's usually about how you hold the yarn, which shows up immediately in photos.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

What are you making right now?

Thirty minutes, free, on your project and whatever's going wrong with it.

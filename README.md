## Business description (Stripe, directories…)

```
Coaching in knitting and crochet, delivered online: small live sessions and one-on-one video coaching covering learning to knit, crochet fundamentals, knitting garments that fit including gauge and sizing, and colorwork and finishing techniques. Students follow a 6 to 12 week program with photo reviews of their work. No yarn, patterns, needles or physical products are sold or shipped. Services are sold as month-to-month subscriptions from $25 to $135 per month, cancellable at any time. Site: elvaara.shop
```

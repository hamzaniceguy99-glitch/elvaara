How we work

Small fixes, caught early

Most knitting problems are mechanical and specific: yarn held too tightly, a stitch mounted backwards, a needle size that doesn't match the yarn. They're hard to describe in words and instantly visible in a close-up photo.

So every program runs on weekly photos. Problems get caught in week two rather than discovered when a sweater is finished and doesn't fit, which is the expensive version.

Gauge gets more attention here than anywhere else, because it decides whether a garment fits. A properly washed swatch is dull and it saves entire projects.
